Publishing Pipeline

How It Works

A transparent four-stage lifecycle from author submission to global discovery.

1

Submit

Students and researchers submit manuscripts with metadata, keywords, and co-author rosters.

2

Review

Designated faculty members examine methodologies, verify novelty, and provide feedback.

3

Approve

Evaluated papers receive formal status updates and reviewer sign-off.

4

Discover

Approved works are indexed into the searchable institutional knowledge base for download.
